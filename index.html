Roaming

Take your data with you.

Short-term data passes covering 96 countries — every EU and Caribbean nation included — across the Americas, Europe, Asia, the Middle East, Africa and Oceania. Pick a continent, then a destination, to see pricing, included calls & texts, and network generation.

What happens when your data runs out?

Your line is never disconnected when a roaming allowance is used up. Instead, data automatically drops to 4G only, with speeds throttled to roughly 64 kbps or lower, until you renew your current plan, purchase a new one, or your existing plan expires — at which point full-speed access resumes.

Any outgoing calls or SMS made beyond your included allowance during this period are billed at standard international roaming rates, while data access remains unavailable until a new plan is activated.

Incoming SMS is always free, on every plan, in every destination.
